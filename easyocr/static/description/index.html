What it does

Reads the document

Supplier, tax number, document number, dates, totals and lines are read from the file, and shown for review and correction before any bill is made.

Remembers each supplier

A supplier that sends the same layout every month only has to be set up once. Its boxes are saved as a template, so the same fields are read again and the review gets shorter over time.

Builds the entry

One click turns the document into a supplier bill, with its lines and its taxes. The supplier is matched by tax number, or created from what was read. A credit note becomes a vendor credit note instead.

Reads many at once

Drop a whole folder and let it run. Each document keeps its own state, so the ones that are ready can be turned into bills while the rest are still going.

Tells your Odoo when it is done

The reading does not have to be watched: the service calls back when a document is ready, and every call is written down so you can see what came in, and when.

Spanish taxes as they are

IVA, the equivalence surcharge and IRPF arrive on the lines, and a rectificativa becomes a vendor credit note with its entries the other way round.

Who it is for

Accounting, in house or for your clients

The supplier invoices of the month stop being typed in: they are dropped as files, checked where they were read, and turned into bills. Those that arrive by email as a PDF with text cost nothing to read, which is most of them.

Purchasing and administration

Whoever receives the invoice can put it in Odoo the moment it arrives, instead of holding it for someone else to key in. A scan needs the reading service; a PDF that already carries its text does not.

The one who pays and photographs the receipt

A receipt paid out of pocket is photographed on the phone and leaves the ticket in Odoo, as an employee expense if the settings say so, for whoever approves expenses. Nobody approves their own expense.

Install it

  1. Open Apps in your Odoo and search for EasyOCR.
  2. Press Install. It asks for nothing you do not already have: Odoo Community 16.0, 17.0, 18.0, 19.0 or 20.0, no Enterprise, and nothing else to buy.
  3. Step 3 is only for scans and photographs: a PDF that already carries its text is read without configuring anything. Open Settings, look for EasyOCR, and paste the address of the reading service and your API key. The key comes with an EasyOCR account, and accounts are self-service: the sign-up at app.easyocr.es/register starts on a free plan (0 EUR a month, 10 pages a month, no card). The button beside them checks the key without spending a reading.
  4. Open the EasyOCR menu, drop a document, and read what came back.

The module is translated into Spanish, Catalan, Galician, German, French, Italian and Portuguese besides English, and the reading is returned in the language of the screen.

What it does not do

  • Scans and photographs are not read without the service. A PDF that carries its text is.
  • IGIC, the Canary Islands tax, is not handled. IVA, the equivalence surcharge and IRPF are.
  • XML invoices (UBL, CII, Factur-X) are left to Odoo, which already imports them without this module.
  • A rate your company has no purchase tax for leaves the line without tax, so it is worth a look before validating the bill.

It is listed here because finding it out afterwards is worse. A reading that fails is not lost either: the document keeps its state and the reason, and can be sent again.
